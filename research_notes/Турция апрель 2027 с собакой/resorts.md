# Turkey, second half of April 2027: top family resorts that accept a small dog

Travellers: 2 adults + children aged 10, 8 and 2 + a toy poodle under 7 kg. 7 nights. Budget: "only top", not saving. Russian citizens flying from Sochi; booking through Russian tour operators (Anex, Pegas, Coral, Fun&Sun, Biblio Globus) or direct.

**How these notes were made (read first):** the research was done on **2026-11-06** and relies only on web-search result summaries.
- Direct page fetches were blocked by the session's network policy (DNS failure or proxy 403) for pegasus.ru, vc.ru, otpusk.pro, kolokoltravel.ru and others. So official hotel pages and PDFs are cited through what the search engine showed of them, not from a full read.
- Partway through, the shared web-search budget for the session ran out (200 calls). As a result, **family facilities (kids-club ages, heated and indoor pools, aquaparks), April opening dates for most Antalya hotels, Istanbul hotels and all prices could not be checked.** Anything not verified is marked **[unverified]** or **[general knowledge]**.
- Pet policies are as reported on the date above. They change often, and third-party booking sites (Booking, Hotels.com, Expedia, lastminute, BringFido) often contradict hotel documents. **Get the hotel's written confirmation for the dog before paying.**

Price labels: **[observed 2026]**, **[2027 listing]**, **[estimate]**. No observed 2026 prices or 2027 listings could be collected because search ran out. **Every price below is an [estimate]**: my own judgement from typical April price levels for these hotels up to mid-2026, not a quote.

## 1. Bottom line

1. Most top-tier UAI resorts in Belek, Lara and Kemer **do not accept dogs.** Rixos (official FAQs), Maxx Royal, Gloria, Regnum (no information found), Titanic, Calista, Susesi, Ela, Cornelia, Voyage, Delphin, Liberty Lara, NG Phaselis, Kaya Palazzo, Sirene, Cullinan and Concorde all say no or show no sign of accepting dogs. See section 4.
2. Top-level options where a dog is **officially or semi-officially possible:**
   - **Antalya:** Kempinski The Dome Belek (villas only), Barut Hemera in Side (designated rooms), Selectum Family Resort Belek ("Nature Area").
   - **Bodrum:** Lujo Bodrum (UAI, but max 6 kg and opens late April), Six Senses Kaplankaya, Kempinski Barbaros Bay, Swissôtel Bodrum Beach, The Bodrum EDITION.
3. **For April with a 2-year-old, the Antalya region is the stronger choice.**
   - Hotels there run year-round or from early spring.
   - There are plenty of direct flights from Sochi to Antalya.
   - UAI is standard.
   - Bodrum luxury hotels open seasonally (mid/late April or even May), mostly sell half board or B&B rather than UAI, and Sochi–Bodrum flights in April are scarce **[general knowledge, unverified]**.

## 5. Booking from Russia with a dog

- **The hotel must confirm in advance.** Barut Hemera states explicitly that you may not arrive with a pet without prior confirmation, and only if a pet room is free. Lujo requires its pet form before arrival. The Dome requires a signed pet agreement.
- **Through a tour operator** (Anex, Pegas, Coral, Fun&Sun, Biblio Globus), the dog is not part of the package price. Add a request to the booking such as «размещение с собакой (той-пудель, X кг), номер категории Pet Friendly», and wait for the operator's written hotel confirmation before paying in full. The pet fee is normally paid at the hotel at check-in (Hemera: €25/day payable at check-in per a Google listing). **[General practice; verify with the chosen operator.]**
- **Booking direct** for Lujo, Kempinski or Six Senses is often simpler: one email thread with the hotel's reservations team, and the pet agreement signed in advance.
- **Documents at check-in:** vaccination records. Kempinski Barbaros Bay may ask for vet records at any time, and R-Express notes that Turkish hotels require vaccination documents at check-in.
- **Flights from Sochi:** check the airline's pet-in-cabin rules separately. Charter flights inside tour packages often restrict pets. The usual in-cabin limit is about 8 kg including the carrier **[general knowledge]**.
- Turkey's entry rules for the dog (chip, rabies vaccination, possibly a titer test) and Russia's re-entry rules were **not researched here**.

## 6. Dates and pricing notes for the second half of April 2027

- Russian May holidays start on 1 May 2027. In Turkey, 23 April is Children's Day (a public holiday). Prices usually rise in the last days of April, so **about 15–22 April or 16–23 April is likely cheaper than 24 April – 1 May [general knowledge; estimate]**.
- Bodrum hotels open late (Lujo: 19–25 April in past years; EDITION: 16 April in 2026), so Bodrum dates should start no earlier than about 25 April.
- Antalya sea temperature in April is about 17–19 °C (from the brief). Heated outdoor pools and indoor pools matter: verify per hotel; **not verified this session.**
- **Price figures:** all are [estimate]. Russian aggregators (Level.Travel, Travelata, Onlinetours) usually show 2027 early-booking prices from late autumn to winter. Re-check them when booking opens.
