## 1. Gardaland calendar: is 2027 published, what happened in 2022–2026, and how likely is the park to be open in late March – mid April 2027?

### Takeaway
The 2027 summer-season calendar is **not published** (as of 2026-11-03). Only Magic Winter, 5 Dec 2026 – 6 Jan 2027, is confirmed. Between 2022 and 2026 the park always opened on a **Saturday 8 or 15 days before Easter**, never earlier than 23 March, and then ran **every day** with 10:00–17:00/18:00 hours. With Easter on 28 March 2027, my inference is that opening on Sat 20 March 2027 is most likely, and opening by Easter Saturday 27 March is near-certain.

### Inferences (NOT FACT: my reasoning from the 2022–2026 pattern)
- **Pattern:** opening is a Saturday 8 or 15 days before Easter, and never before about 23 March in this sample. For 2027, "8 days before" gives **Sat 20 March 2027**. "15 days before" gives Sat 13 March, which is earlier than any recent opening and unlikely. Bavaria's Easter school holidays start 22 March 2027 (section 9), which supports a 20 March opening.
- Probabilities that the park is open:
  - **Sat 20 – Thu 25 March 2027:** about **55–65%**. The park may open 20 March, or only for the Easter weekend.
  - **Good Friday 26 March – Easter Monday 29 March 2027:** about **90–95%**. The Easter weekend plus German/Austrian holidays is a peak the park has never missed in the sample.
  - **Any given day from 30 March to about 18 April 2027:** about **85–90%** per day once the season has started. The 2024 and 2026 precedent is "every day", but I could not confirm that no weekday closures were ever scheduled.
  - **Expected hours** (inference from 2024/2026): about 10:00–17:00 on weekdays, 10:00–18:00 at weekends and on Easter days.
- **Crowds:** busiest roughly 26 March – 3 April 2027 (Easter plus Bavaria/BW holidays). **Weekdays 6–16 April are probably the best Gardaland days**: low-season dynamic prices, smaller queues, park very likely open.
- The calendar is usually announced in winter (around January–February; inference from coverage timing). Recheck gardaland.it about January 2027 before booking non-refundable items.

## 3. Height restrictions: what the 10-, 8- and 2-year-old can ride

### Takeaway
- **10-year-old** (if ≥140 cm): can ride nearly everything, including Raptor and Oblivion (both 140 cm).
- **8-year-old** (about 125–135 cm): Shaman (120), Mammut (110), Jungle Rapids (110–120) and most family rides, but not the 140 cm coasters.
- **2-year-old** (85–95 cm): mostly limited to **Peppa Pig Land** (some rides from 80 cm) and the gentle Fantasy Kingdom / Prezzemolo-area rides. Parents report this fills only about a morning.

### Cited Findings
- **Raptor:** min **140 cm**, max 195 cm — [parchi-divertimento.com Raptor](https://www.parchi-divertimento.com/gardaland/en/rides/raptor)
- **Oblivion – The Black Hole:** min **140 cm**, max 195 cm — [parchi-divertimento.com Oblivion](https://www.parchi-divertimento.com/gardaland/en/rides/oblivion-the-black-hole)
- **Blue Tornado:** min **140 cm**, max 200 cm (one older guide says 130) — [GardalandPlusFan limits](https://gardalandplusfan.com/limitazioni-di-sicurezza/)
- **Shaman:** min **120 cm**, max 205 cm — [GardalandPlusFan](https://gardalandplusfan.com/limitazioni-di-sicurezza/)
- **Mammut:** min **110 cm** per the fan site; another guide says 130 cm (conflict) — [GardalandPlusFan](https://gardalandplusfan.com/limitazioni-di-sicurezza/); [thebettervacation rides](https://thebettervacation.com/it/milan/gardaland-rides/)
- **Jungle Rapids:** **110 or 120 cm** (sources conflict); the official map PDF appears to show 120 — [Gardaland map PDF](https://www.gardaland.it/media/pubhskhw/mappa-anteprima-gardaland.pdf); [verona-tickets rides](https://www.verona-tickets.com/gardaland-park/rides/)
- **Peppa Pig Land:**
  - Grandpa Pig's train and the balloon ride are listed as usable by children **under 90 cm**, with the balloon needing **at least 80 cm** (presumably accompanied) — [Passaporto di Famiglia](https://www.passaportodifamiglia.com/en/italy/veneto-en/gardaland-where-it-is-practical-info-attractions-age-group/)

- **Family reviews:**
  - "Peppa Pig land was a hit with a 2 year old and several other rides were suitable" — [Mumsnet: Gardaland with toddlers in spring](https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/holidays/3328878-Gardaland-with-Toddlers-in-the-spring)

### Inferences
- A good split for one day is two groups: one adult with the 10- and 8-year-old on the thrill rides (Shaman, Mammut, Jungle Rapids; Raptor, Oblivion and Blue Tornado for the 10-year-old if tall enough), and one adult with the toddler (Peppa Pig Land, Prezzemolo Land, shows). Rejoin for family rides.
- The parent-swap / "baby switch" option is probably available but **was not verified**.

## 11. Twelve-day interest: is one base in Garda enough for children aged 10, 8 and 2 in early spring? Second base?

### Takeaway
**Yes, with caveats.** The area offers about **9–11 distinct day-outings** that are open in this window: Gardaland (1–2 days), SEA LIFE, Venice, Verona, Sigurtà tulips, Natura Viva, Movieland (if open), ferry/Sirmione, Monte Baldo (if open), Aquardens, and lake-town strolls. That covers 12 days at a relaxed pace with 2–3 rest or rain days.

The weak points:
- the toddler's ride options at Gardaland are small;
- about one in three April days has rain;
- the lake is too cold for beach play;
- Easter week (26 March – 4 April) is crowded, with peak prices.

A second base adds little. The one case for it is a single Venice night to spare the 2-year-old a 12-hour round trip.

### Cited Findings
- The Peppa Pig + Fantasy Kingdom toddler zone fills about a morning or afternoon; the Peppa area has 3–4 rides with queues — [thebettervacation](https://thebettervacation.com/milan/peppa-pig-land-gardaland-park/); [Tripadvisor review](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g644289-d568445-r1072599247-Gardaland_Park-Castelnuovo_del_Garda_Province_of_Verona_Veneto.html)
- Opening windows confirming what is available in early spring:
  - Gardaland from late March — section 1.
  - Natura Viva daily from 28 Feb — section 4.
  - Sigurtà from 7 March, tulips mid-March to May — section 4.
  - Movieland from 2 April (2026) — section 4.
  - Monte Baldo from 4 April (2026) — section 4.
  - Ferries' spring timetable from 1 April (2026) — section 6.
  - LEGOLAND Water Park only from 30 May — section 1.
- About 8–11 rain days in April; the lake is chilly — section 5.
- The 2027 Venice fee applies at Easter and on weekends; mid-week days are free — section 7.
- One guide advises staying overnight in Venice rather than a summer day trip with a toddler — [22places](https://www.22places.com/venice-with-kids/)

### Inferences
**Sample 12-day skeleton** (arrival around 2–3 April to avoid the Easter peak; my construction, adjust to flights):

| Day | Plan |
|---|---|
| 1 | Arrival, settle in, Peschiera ramparts walk and playground |
| 2 | Gardaland (weekday) |
| 3 | Easy day: ferry Peschiera → Sirmione (castle, gelato) |
| 4 | Sigurtà tulips (car or bus) |
| 5 | Venice (Tue–Thu fee-free day), train |
| 6 | Rest / Lazise lakefront + playground |
| 7 | Parco Natura Viva (safari by car) |
| 8 | Gardaland day 2 + SEA LIFE (2-day combo) |
| 9 | Verona half-day by train |
| 10 | Monte Baldo cable car + Malcesine (car) |
| 11 | Movieland for the older two with one parent; Aquardens or rest for the toddler with the other; or a rain-day swap |
| 12 | Bardolino/Garda by ferry, departure prep |

- **Rain plan** (swap freely): SEA LIFE, Aquardens kids' thermal pools, Verona museums.
- **Dates:**
  - Option A, **2–14 April 2027**: avoids the Easter peak and the Bavaria/BW holidays, maximises open attractions (Movieland, Monte Baldo, ferries on the spring timetable), and offers many fee-free Venice weekdays. Slightly warmer and greener; tulips at or near peak.
  - Option B, **around 24 March – 5 April**: catches Easter events, but means higher prices, crowds, the Venice fee on 26–29 March, a reduced ferry timetable before 1 April, possibly closed cable car and Movieland, and colder weather.
- **Second base:**
  - **Venice 1–2 nights** (mid-week): advantages are no day-trip fatigue for the toddler, an early-morning and evening Venice, and overnight guests exempt from the fee (2026 rule). Disadvantages are packing up with three children, Venice hotel prices, and the strollers-and-bridges problem.
  - **Dolomites in early April:** mostly end of ski season and shoulder closures, with little for a 2-year-old. Not recommended (inference).
  - A **one-night Venice stopover at the end** (fly out of VCE/Marco Polo) could work if the flights allow an open-jaw. Flights are researched separately.
- **Pace for a 2-year-old:** one "big" day followed by one "small" day. Gardaland and Venice are the most tiring; never schedule them back to back.
