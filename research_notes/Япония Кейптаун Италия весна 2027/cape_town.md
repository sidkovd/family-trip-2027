## 1. WHALES (critical): is late March – mid April a whale trip?

### Takeaway
**No. April is not a whale trip.** The family site is **correct**: Hermanus/Walker Bay southern right whales are a June–November/early-December phenomenon, peaking August–October. No source I found reports regular southern right sightings in April, and the main Hermanus boat operator runs whale trips only from June to December. In April the realistic whale options are an **occasional, non-guaranteed Bryde's whale** (a year-round resident in False Bay and off Gansbaai) or a stray humpback seen on a general marine-wildlife boat trip, such as the Gansbaai "Marine Big 5" or a Simon's Town cruise. Penguins, seals and dolphins are the dependable sightings. The West Coast humpback "super-groups" peak around **November** and run into the austral summer (December–January). They are not an April phenomenon.

### Cited Findings
**Southern right whale (SRW) season at Hermanus/Walker Bay**
- Hermanus whale season runs "every year, between June and early December". — [Hermanus Whale Festival](https://hermanuswhalefestival.co.za/whale-season-in-hermanus-why-nows-the-best-time-to-visit/)
- SRW can be seen in Walker Bay for about 6 months each year, from early June to late November/early December. Peak timing is given as August to late October, late September/early October, or "September and October by a wide margin", depending on the source. Calving takes place in August–September, and males arrive for mating in October, when numbers peak. — search synthesis of [Africa Freak](https://africafreak.com/hermanus-whale-watching), [Windsor Hotel](https://windsorhotel.co.za/whale-watching/), [Wikipedia: Hermanus](https://en.Wikipedia.com/wiki/Hermanus) (whales "usually depart in early December"), and [Audley Travel](https://www.audleytravel.com/south-africa/places-to-go/hermanus)
- Southern Right Charters, the main Hermanus boat operator, says its whale watching season is **June to December**. Peak is usually cited as August–October. "The first southern rights show up in June, numbers drop through November, and the last leave in December." — [Southern Right Charters](https://www.southernrightcharters.co.za/whale-watching/); [Hermanus Tourism listing](https://hermanus-tourism.co.za/info/southern-right-charters/)
- Early arrivals: a Hermanus Times report says SRW were "spotted near De Kelders and the broader Overstrand area as early as the beginning of May – weeks ahead of the season's typical kick-off around June", with humpback activity also reported. I could not confirm the year of the report. — [Nova News / Hermanus Times](https://novanews.co.za/?p=346102)
- A commercial blog says you "might even spot early arrivals in April or late stragglers in January". This is a low-quality source with no data behind it. — [EcoFlow blog](https://www.ecoflow.com/za/blog/whale-watching-hermanus)
- October 2026 news confirms the season pattern: SRW "return to South Africa, drawing crowds to the coast" (7 Oct 2026). — [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/10/07/southern-right-whales-return-to-south-africa-drawing-crowds-to-the-coast); [Africanews, 6 Oct 2026](https://www.africanews.com/2026/10/06/annual-sightings-of-southern-right-whales-bring-joy-to-south-african-town/)
- Humpbacks at Hermanus are "occasional", and SRW are the regular visitors. Southern Right Charters' 2024 season post gives peak humpback months in the area as **May to August** (the northbound migration). — [Southern Right Charters, 2024 season](https://www.southernrightcharters.co.za/blog/whales/hermanus-whale-watching-season-2024/); [EcoFlow](https://www.ecoflow.com/za/blog/whale-watching-hermanus)
- SRW monitoring in South Africa was started in 1969 by Peter Best, who founded the Whale Unit at the University of Pretoria in 1985. — [Whale Coast info](https://whalecoast.info/attraction/whale-watching-on-the-cape-whale-coast/)

**Other whales present in late March – April**
- **Bryde's whales** are "all-year-round residents" of False Bay. The migratory southern rights and humpbacks "are the stars of the whale watching tours". — [Simon's Town Boat Company listing (What's On)](https://whatsoninjoburg.com/event/simons-town-boat-company)
- False Bay marine guides disagree on Bryde's whales. One says they are present year-round with numbers peaking in summer and autumn. Another calls them "sporadic" in the bay. — search synthesis of [Dive Team](https://diveteam.co.za/blogs/news/whale-season-in-cape-town-a-guide-to-spotting-the-giants-of-false-bay), [Ultimate Animals](https://ultimate-animals.com/marine-wildlife-seasons-in-false-bay/), and [SA-Venues](https://www.sa-venues.com/attractionswc/cape-peninsula-whales.php) (I could not tell which page made which claim)
- capetown.org lists "January to May: Humpback and Bryde's whales" for False Bay. Another guide says humpbacks can be seen feeding in Table Bay in summer (February–March) from Waterfront boats. Other guides put False Bay humpbacks at June–September. — [capetown.org](https://capetown.org/whale-watching-in-the-cape-town-south-africa-area/); [Ultimate Animals](https://ultimate-animals.com/marine-wildlife-seasons-in-false-bay/); [Getaway](https://www.getaway.co.za/travel/the-best-land-based-whale-watching-in-cape-town/)
- Gansbaai: "from January to May, after southern right whales have departed", Marine Dynamics runs eco cruises and Marine Big 5 trips to Dyer Island and Geyser Rock, where "you might encounter the graceful Humpback and Bryde's whales". — [Xplorio Gansbaai](https://xplorio.com/gansbaai/marine-dynamics-whale-tours/); [Hermanus Tourism: Marine Dynamics](https://hermanus-tourism.co.za/info/marine-dynamics-whale-tours/)

That was in season, not April. — [CapeTownEtc](https://www.capetownetc.com/news/killer-whales-reach-false-bay-after-south-coast-sightings/)

**West Coast humpback "super-groups"**
- Super-groups of 20+ feeding humpbacks form "during the austral summer" in the southern Benguela off the west coast. A 2025 *Marine Mammal Science* paper collated **239 super-groups from July 2015 to June 2022**. — [University of Pretoria repository](https://repository.up.ac.za/items/70ac6126-1572-4abc-af78-de15773f8d7d/full)
- South African government: feeding frenzies occur in spring and summer, **peaking in November**, and have been encountered consistently since 2014. — [gov.za](https://www.gov.za/node/794193); [IOL 2019](https://iol.co.za/news/south-africa/2019-12-13-government-calls-for-protection-of-whale-super-groups-off-west-coast/)
- Super-groups occur between St Helena Bay and Cape Point. A large phytoplankton bloom occurred about a month before each super-group. — [UCT News 2021](https://news.uct.ac.za/article/-2021-10-22-explaining-the-super-groups-of-humpback-whales-on-the-west-coast-of-south-africa-1)
- Search coverage also mentions a photographer counting **304 whales on 30 December 2025** (late in the season). I could not determine which of these sources carried that claim. — [GroundUp](https://groundup.news/article/humpback-whale-supergroups-return-to-cape-towns-west-coast/); [AP via Las Vegas Sun, Jul 2026](https://lasvegassun.com/news/2026/jul/25/whale-supergroups-show-numbers-are-rebounding-from/)

### Inferences
- **Verdict on the family's site:** the statement "April is outside the main whale season" is **verified**. The site's softer claim that feeding whales appear near Cape Town in February–April is only weakly supported (capetown.org says "January–May humpback & Bryde's" for False Bay, and one guide puts Table Bay humpbacks in February–March). The super-group literature puts the peak in **November**, extending into summer (December–January), not April. Present any April sighting as luck.
- **Rough April odds (judgement, not a published statistic):** SRW from land at Hermanus: **very low**. On a Gansbaai Marine Big 5 boat trip: penguins and seals near-certain, dolphins likely, a Bryde's whale possible but not likely enough to promise. Land-based False Bay sightings: low, and opportunistic.
- **Best "marine" plan for April with a 2-year-old:** Boulders Beach and Stony Point penguins (land-based, guaranteed), plus one Marine Dynamics Big 5 trip from Gansbaai (about 40 min beyond Hermanus). The toddler goes free, all ages are accepted if seas allow, and there is a chance of a Bryde's whale. A Hout Bay seal cruise (45 min) is the cheap, short toddler-friendly alternative.
- **If whales are non-negotiable**, the trip must move to **mid-August to October** (SRW peak; humpback super-groups build from October).

### Gaps
- No quantitative April sighting rate (percentage of trips with whales) was found for any operator. Operators' own sighting logs or research data (e.g. MRI Whale Unit, Dyer Island Conservation Trust) were not reachable in this session.
- Marine Dynamics' 2027 prices and the exact international child rate need confirming. Rates were "valid until 31 Dec 2026".
- Simon's Town Boat Company's April schedule, child prices and minimum age: not found.

## 3. TABLE MOUNTAIN: Aerial Cableway

### Takeaway
2026 prices (third-party, consistent across sources): **R475 adult return online (R530 at the ticket office), R230 child 4–17, under 4 free** (**2026 reference**). The annual maintenance shutdown is in **late July/August** (2026: 27 Jul – 9 Aug), so **it does not affect late March – April**. The real risk is **wind or cloud closures, 60–90 days per year**. Keep 2–3 flexible weather windows and buy online to skip the ticket-office queue.

### Cited Findings
- Adult return **R475 online / R530 ticket office**; child (4–17) **R230**; **under 4 free**. — [Planet Whitley](https://planetwhitley.com/the-table-mountain-aerial-cableway-guide-ticket-prices-hours-and-what-to-see/); [CapeTrek (2026)](https://capetrek.co.za/table-mountain-cable-car-price/). One ticket site converts R475 ≈ $29.50 and R530 ≈ $32.90 and notes that **online tickets skip the office queue**. — [Cape Town City Guide](https://capetowncityguide.com/table-mountain-tickets/). Older figures (R380/R395 adult, R190/R200 child) are outdated. — **2026 reference**
- The official ticket page did not show amounts in the search extract. It states the company "reserves the right to increase ticket prices and change operating times without prior notice". Hours on a 28 Oct 2026 snapshot: **first car up 08:30, last up 18:00, last down 19:00**. — [tablemountain.net ticket prices](https://www.tablemountain.net/plan-your-visit/ticket-prices/)
- **2026 maintenance shutdown: Mon 27 July – Sun 9 August 2026**, reopening 10 August. The 2025 shutdown ran 28 July – 3 August 2025. ### Inferences
- Family cost: 2×R475 + 2×R230 + toddler free = **R1,410 online** (**estimate based on 2026 reference**). At the ticket office it would be about R1,520+.
- 60–90 closure days a year is about 16–25% of days (my arithmetic). Summer south-easters are worse than late March/April. Plan Table Mountain as a "first clear, calm morning" activity with 2–3 candidate days in the Cape Town block. Check the cableway's live status or app before driving up.
- Do **not** hike up with the 2-year-old; use the cableway (see the Safety section on trail muggings).
- The 2027 shutdown is expected in July/August by pattern, but 2027 dates are not yet published.

## 4. KIRSTENBOSCH and other kid attractions; malaria-free safari options

### Takeaway
Kirstenbosch is cheap for the family (international adult **R270**, child 6–17 **R40**, under 6 free; cashless; online via Webtickets). Strong toddler-friendly options: Boulders and Stony Point penguins, the Kirstenbosch Tree Canopy walkway area and lawns, Two Oceans Aquarium (under 4 free), Babylonstoren (R150 adult, **under 18 free**), the Hout Bay seal cruise (about 45 min, cheap) and Giraffe House or Cheetah Outreach near Stellenbosch. For a malaria-free safari, **Aquila is the only option where the 2-year-old can join game drives (no minimum age)**. **Inverdoorn and Sanbona exclude under-4s from game drives**, and Sanbona's Gondwana Family Lodge offers childminding instead.

## 5. CAR RENTAL: vehicle, cover, child seats, licence, deposit, fuel, tolls

### Takeaway
A 7-seater (Toyota Fortuner class) at Cape Town is roughly **US$78–107/day before zero-excess cover**. ## 10. WEATHER & OCEAN: late March – mid April

### Takeaway
April is mild and often the calmest, most pleasant month. Highs are about **22–23 °C** and lows about **12–14 °C**. Rain is about **38–45 mm over roughly 7–8 days** and increases toward May. Late March is a bit warmer and drier. Sea temperature is given as **about 17–19 °C** by climate sites. In practice the **Atlantic side (Clifton/Camps Bay) is usually much colder** than **False Bay (Muizenberg, Boulders, Fish Hoek)**, so **False Bay is the realistic place for kids to swim and paddle**.

## 11. HOLIDAYS & CROWDS: Easter 2027 and Western Cape school holidays

### Takeaway
2027 school **Term 1 ends on 19 March** and **Term 2 starts on 6 April** (national calendar, which applies to the Western Cape). So **about 20 March – 5 April 2027 is the SA school holiday**, and it **coincides with Easter**: Good Friday 26 March, Family Day 29 March. Expect higher prices, booked-out family accommodation, and queues at Boulders, the cableway and Cape Point in that window. **From about 6 April onward it is notably quieter.**

## 12. HEALTH: malaria, yellow fever via Addis Ababa, tap water

### Takeaway
The Western Cape (Cape Town, Hermanus, Aquila, Inverdoorn, Sanbona) is **malaria-free**. South Africa requires a **yellow fever certificate from travellers over 1 year old who transited for more than 12 hours** through an airport in a yellow-fever-risk country. **Ethiopia is listed as a risk country** by a third-party compilation, so a **layover of 12 h or more in Addis Ababa would trigger the requirement for all three children** (ages 2, 8, 10) and the adults. A short connection (under 12 h) is not covered by the rule as written.

### Cited Findings
- South African rule: a valid yellow fever certificate is required from travellers "in transit exceeding 12 hours, through the airport of a country with risk of yellow fever transmission". It applies to citizens and non-citizens over 1 year of age. — [DIRCO (SA High Commission Canberra)](https://dirco.gov.za/canberra/yellow-fever/); [SAHC Australia](https://sahc.org.au/yellow-fever.htm)
- A third-party list of countries SA considers risk countries includes **Ethiopia**: travellers without a valid certificate "will be refused entry". — [African Overland Tours](https://africanoverlandtours.com/blog/guide-to-yellow-fever-and-your-southern-africa-trip-clearing-up-the-confusion/)
- Ethiopia itself requires proof of vaccination for travellers aged 9 months+ arriving from risk countries or after 12+ h of transit through one. — [SafariFind](https://safarifind.com/blog/what-health-rules-apply-in-ethiopia-to-south-africans-2026)
- Anecdote: a frequent Addis transit passenger was never asked for a certificate, even on 12+ h layovers. This does not change the written rule. — [VoyageForum](https://voyageforum.com/en/forum/layover-in-ethiopia-en-route-to-south-africa-yellow-fever-d10661646/)
- Malaria-free: Inverdoorn is "a malaria-free safari destination", and Sanbona offers a safari "in a malaria-free area". — [African Budget Safaris](https://www.africanbudgetsafaris.com/locations/inverdoorn-game-reserve/); [Tourism Tattler](https://www.tourismtattler.com/?p=77215)

### Inferences
- When the flights researcher picks Ethiopian Airlines via ADD (or Kenya Airways via NBO), **choose connections under 12 h**, or vaccinate everyone over 1 year at least 10 days before departure (general WHO rule, not verified this session). Note that the 2-year-old is also covered by the "over 1 year" rule. Routes via Dubai, Doha or Istanbul do not involve yellow-fever countries.
- Cape Town tap water is generally treated and drinkable (general knowledge, not verified this session). The 2026 drought caution may mean taste or pressure changes, not a safety issue.
